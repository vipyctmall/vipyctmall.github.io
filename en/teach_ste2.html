VIPYCTMALL SHOPPING GUIDE

Complete Shopping Guide

01 MALL LOGIN

Log in to the marketplace

  • Enter your registered username and login password.
  • Verify the information, then select “Log in” to enter Higo.
  • Create your account and shipping address first if they are not ready.
Use your login password here, not the payment password.
02 PRODUCT CATEGORY

Open the US Direct Shipping category

  • Select “Products” from the top navigation.
  • Choose “Self-operated Products” → “US Direct Shipping” in the left filter.
  • Select the product you want from the product list.
Product availability and prices may change on the official marketplace.
03 PRODUCT DETAILS

Set the quantity and buy now

  • Confirm the product name, price and details.
  • Use “+” and “−” to adjust the quantity; this guide shows 5.
  • Select “Buy Now” after confirming the quantity.
Confirm the correct product and quantity for your needs.
04 SHIPPING

Confirm shipping and address

  • Choose “Mail delivery” as the shipping method.
  • Verify the recipient name, phone, email and address.
  • Check subtotal, shipping and total, then select “Checkout”.
Return to your account page to correct the address when necessary.
05 PAYMENT METHOD

Choose aggregated payment and submit

  • Review the product total, shipping fee and amount due.
  • Select “Aggregated Payment” as the payment method.
  • Enter the payment password created during registration, confirm the address and submit the order.
This is the payment password, which is different from the login password.
06 CARD PAYMENT

Complete card payment

  • Confirm the order amount and payment currency.
  • Enter the card number, expiry date, security code and cardholder name.
  • Review the details and submit the payment.
The third-party payment page may change; follow the current page shown.
This guide is for operational reference only. Product prices, shipping fees, payment screens and button names may change with the official marketplace, region settings or third-party payment provider. Follow the page displayed when placing your order.
